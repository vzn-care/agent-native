# Use HyperLight as the Shopify Pilot

HyperLight at `https://hyperlightoptics.com/` is the named production pilot merchant and Store Connection. The launch remains bounded to a selected 10–25 adult frame variants, the United States USD market, domestic fulfillment, and the approved pilot lens catalog even though the public storefront exposes a broader international and product catalog. HyperLight must still complete Store Certification, catalog and Frame Optical Profile approval, test-order and refund authority, inventory routing, and operational-owner assignment before live Rx activation.
