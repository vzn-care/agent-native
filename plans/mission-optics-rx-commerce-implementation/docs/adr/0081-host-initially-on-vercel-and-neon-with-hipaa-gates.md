# Host Initially on Vercel and Neon With HIPAA Gates

`vzn-care/vznrx` initially runs on Vercel with a dedicated Neon Postgres data plane and separate development, staging, and production environments. Before any production PHI enters either provider, Mission must execute and retain the applicable Business Associate Agreements, enable each provider's HIPAA offering on the exact covered team and projects, document covered services and shared-responsibility configuration, restrict access, verify audit and incident procedures, and complete a security and privacy launch review. PHI remains prohibited from logs, traces, analytics, build artifacts, schema descriptions, and provider metadata. Existing Mission systems integrate through versioned actions, events, or APIs rather than direct database writes.
