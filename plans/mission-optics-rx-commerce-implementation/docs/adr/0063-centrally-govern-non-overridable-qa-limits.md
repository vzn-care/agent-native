# Centrally Govern Non-Overridable QA Limits

Every Production Attempt receives a QA Inspection under a versioned Mission-controlled Quality Policy. Mission's authorized optical and quality governance process defines the specific numeric and categorical Critical QA Limits, the required measurement methods, and the evidence used to establish them. No merchant, lab, administrator, manager, or role configuration may broaden or waive those limits. Objective failures beyond a Critical QA Limit require rework, a new Production Attempt, or an incident disposition. A configured QA Manager may issue a case-specific QA Exception Approval only within an explicitly documented noncritical tolerance band, recording the check, measured result, permitted tolerance, reason, approver, and exact attempt.
