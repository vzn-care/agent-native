# End Mission Password Access at the Scale Gate

Mission must enable approved-domain Google Workspace SSO before onboarding the second live merchant or declaring VZNRX generally available, whichever occurs first. At that gate, ordinary Mission workforce password sign-in is disabled. Mission may retain at most two separately governed emergency accounts protected by phishing-resistant MFA, offline recovery procedures, immediate-use alerts, periodic review, and access testing. Merchant and shopper identity paths remain unchanged.
