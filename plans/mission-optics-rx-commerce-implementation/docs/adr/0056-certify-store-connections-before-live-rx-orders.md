# Certify Store Connections Before Live Rx Orders

A Store Connection cannot process live Rx orders until it passes Store Certification. Certification covers automated sandbox tests for configuration, cart construction, checkout validation, discount and tax behavior, payment, refunds, webhook delivery and replay, fulfillment, and reconciliation, plus at least one end-to-end order through the merchant's real theme and enabled accelerated-checkout paths. Mission verifies catalog bindings, validation controls, permissions, subscriptions, and fulfillment settings, then records Mission approval, merchant acceptance, and the certified capability fingerprint. Later drift that removes a required safety control suspends new configurations while Mission continues servicing existing paid obligations.
