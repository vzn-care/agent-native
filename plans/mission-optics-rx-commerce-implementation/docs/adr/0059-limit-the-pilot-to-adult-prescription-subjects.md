# Limit the Pilot to Adult Prescription Subjects

The first production pilot accepts only Prescription Subjects aged 18 or older under a versioned Subject Eligibility Policy. Mission retains the Subject Representative model because an adult may authorize another person to assist, and the same architecture can later support verified guardian workflows. Minor support remains disabled until a separate activation review approves guardian authority and identity verification, consent and authorization, communications, prescription validity, fitting workflows, retention, and merchant policy.
