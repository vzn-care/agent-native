# Separate Merchant Organizations From Store Connections

Mission models a Merchant Organization independently from its Store Connections. One organization may connect multiple Shopify stores and future commerce channels, while each connection retains separate credentials, Store Certification, capability fingerprint, market, currency, catalog bindings, and external order namespace. Organization-level program templates may feed store-specific published Merchant Program Configurations with only permitted overrides. Authorized organization staff may receive cross-store commercial and operational reporting, but organization membership alone never grants protected prescription access. Platform store and customer identifiers remain external bindings rather than canonical Mission identities.
