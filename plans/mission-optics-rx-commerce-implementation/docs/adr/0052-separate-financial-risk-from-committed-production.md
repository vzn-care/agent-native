# Separate Financial Risk From Committed Production

An authoritative Financial Risk Event immediately revokes Financial Clearance and blocks Production Release when it arrives before Production Commitment. After Production Commitment, the event does not automatically interrupt, discard, or mutate the active Production Attempt; the lab completes or safely dispositions the work under its operating policy while Mission opens an audited financial incident. The customer's protected optical workflow remains separate from the merchant's commercial dispute. When Mission properly committed production before receiving authoritative notice, merchant responsibility is governed by the commercial agreement. Authorized operators may place a reason-bound manual risk hold for exceptional cases.
