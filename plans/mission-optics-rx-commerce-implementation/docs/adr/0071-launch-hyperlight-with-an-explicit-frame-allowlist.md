# Launch HyperLight With an Explicit Frame Allowlist

HyperLight launches with a Pilot Catalog Allowlist of approximately 12–15 adult, in-stock frame variants selected to exercise representative constructions, materials, eye sizes, shapes, indoor or outdoor or hybrid positioning, lens replacement requirements, and production difficulty. Kids products, gift cards, standalone lens sets, clip-ons, sold-out products, and frames without approved Frame Optical Profiles or reliable physical inventory are excluded. Storefront publication alone never makes a product Rx-enabled.
