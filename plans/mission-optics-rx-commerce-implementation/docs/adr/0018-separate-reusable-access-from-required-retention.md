# Separate Reusable Access from Required Retention

Closing an account or honoring a deletion request immediately revokes future prescription reuse and removes records no longer required, but does not erase evidence that Mission must retain for completed-order, manufacturing, quality, warranty, fraud, contractual, or legal purposes. Retained records are minimized, removed from ordinary portal and analytics use, governed by an approved jurisdiction-aware Retention Policy, and deleted or de-identified when that period ends.
